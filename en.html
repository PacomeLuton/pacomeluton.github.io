Research interests

I have been a PhD student since September 2024 in the Maverick team at the Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann (LJK) in Grenoble. I work on real-time rendering.

I am currently interested in curved rays: rather than deforming the geometry of a scene, I aim to deform the space through which light travels, by bending the rays themselves, while remaining compatible with real-time constraints.

Space and Time

Real-time rendering of a Schwarzschild black hole and orbiting planets, highlighting relativistic effects: light bending, Doppler effects, length contraction, aberration, proper time. Each effect can be toggled separately.
